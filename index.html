What Is Aksikontal?

Aksikontal is an early-stage conceptual framework created by Aliy to organize and explore questions about the physical world through mathematics, logical reasoning, and testable ideas.

Its purpose is to bring related questions and mathematical relationships into one organized project. It explores how concepts such as gravity, motion, energy, electricity, and probability can be described, compared, and investigated.

Aksikontal is a work in progress. Its definitions, equations, and proposed connections may be revised as the project develops and as evidence is examined.

Five Areas of Exploration

01 — Gravity

Exploring gravitational attraction, the relationship between mass and distance, and how gravity is described in established physics.

02 — Motion

Examining how an object's position and velocity change over time, including the role of acceleration.

03 — Energy

Studying energy as a physical quantity and exploring how it is transferred or transformed in physical systems.

04 — Electricity

Investigating electrical quantities such as voltage, current, and resistance, while keeping their definitions distinct from mechanical quantities such as speed.

05 — Probability

Describing uncertainty and possible outcomes with mathematical models, observations, and statistical reasoning.

How the Framework Is Developed

Aksikontal aims to develop its ideas through a careful and transparent process:

1. Define

Explain each variable, quantity, assumption, and equation clearly.

2. Compare

Compare each idea with existing mathematics, physics, and relevant scientific literature.

3. Predict

State what an idea predicts and how that prediction could be checked.

4. Test and Revise

Examine reliable evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, and revise ideas when the results do not support them.

Scientific Status

Aksikontal is currently an independent, early-stage conceptual project. It has not been validated as a new scientific theory.

Some equations and concepts considered in the project are already part of established physics or mathematics. Presenting them in a new framework does not by itself establish a new discovery.

Any claim of a new scientific contribution would require precise definitions, predictions that can be tested, appropriate evidence, comparison with existing theories, and independent scientific scrutiny.

The project welcomes careful questions, corrections, and evidence-based discussion.
